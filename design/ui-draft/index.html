Umami v1 · scoping review · draft 1

UI draft — the whole loop, clickable

One working phone with real data: the 44 curated recipes from v2 and the list builder from ADR 0018 (V1–V11) running live. Copy is the approved da-DK deck from spec §12. Tap through it like a parent would. Pin a comment on anything.

What to look at

  1. Three-minute loop. Første start → pick 5 (or ⚡) → Byg sedlen → Start indkøbet → tick everything. Does anything slow you down?
  2. Servings control (V19). The spec's 1–8 segmented row doesn't fit at 320 pt / XL. Draft shows a 4×2 grid. Try both device toggles.
  3. Tray at 6–7 meals. Target 5, soft cap 7: dots 6–7 render smaller. Try adding an 8th.
  4. Hero images are placeholders (v2's generated art style). Real photography is still a release blocker.
  5. Recipes tab (new in draft 2). Plan · Liste · Opskrifter. The picker is now a tab instead of a modal over Plan; the tray (n af 5 · ⚡ · Byg sedlen) lives at the bottom of the tab. Swapping a meal still opens the picker as a modal.
  6. Basisvarer (changed in draft 2). Skipped rows no longer have a checkbox. One button, Skal købes, moves the item up into its aisle (with Fortryd). A tick always means bought.
  7. Swipe on the list (new in draft 3). Drag a row left: Spring over skips it on this list only (also the way back after Skal købes), Basisvare makes it a personal staple (starts in Basisvarer on every new list; manage under ⚙ → Mine basisvarer), Ret mængde sets your own amount (kept until the recipes change that row). Your own ANDET items get Fjern.
  8. ＋ in the List header (new in draft 3) opens Tilføj en vare; “500g kartofler”, “1,5kg” and “2 stk. løg” all parse.
  9. Tilføj en vare (changed in draft 2). Try “2 gulerødder” on a list that already has carrots: it merges into the row in FRUGT & GRØNT. Unknown items (“bleer”) still go to ANDET.
  10. The list. Merged rows (“Fra 2 retter ⌄”, was a ≡ 2 badge), joined amounts for units that don't convert, rounding. Is it trustworthy at a glance?
  11. In-store is dark. v2's design uses a dark takeover. §10 says "no dark mode" — this is one fixed screen style, not a theme. Keep it?
  12. Sign-in offer appears once on the List after the first finished shop, never in the shop.
  13. Tray at 320 pt + XL text. "Byg sedlen" clips. Known gap, needs a design answer (drop the count label? stack the tray?).
  14. Import (changed in draft 2). Opskrifter → Importér → review with 3 flagged lines → Gem. No sign-in: the recipe is saved on the phone and uploads on first sign-in.
  15. Sign-in from ⚙ → Log ind: any 6 digits; 000000 shows the wrong-code state.

Stubbed in this draft

The ⚙ Mere filter sheet covers cuisine and season only. Share sheet shows the plain-text payload, not the OS sheet. Import uses one canned recipe and canned timings (12 s soften is sped up to 4 s). No swipe gestures — use ⋯. Recipe content is v2's slate, unreviewed.
